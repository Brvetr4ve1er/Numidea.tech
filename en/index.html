    How a project is priced

    There are four ways to bill a project. Each one is the norm somewhere.

    Our model

    Fixed price

    One fixed price for a written scope. If the work overruns, the provider absorbs the difference.

    The norm in Algeria: the Onyxlab barometer prices all development as a fixed fee. It's how we price every project.

    Time and materials

    Billed by the day or the hour. Flexible when the scope moves, hard to budget in advance.

    The norm in France (€480–650 a day for an experienced developer) and the US ($100–149 an hour at agencies).

    Our model

    Retainer

    A monthly fee for an ongoing service: maintenance, improvements, content.

    Common everywhere after launch. It's how our maintenance works: 18,000–60,000 DZD a month.

    Value-based

    The price follows what the project earns, not the time it takes.

    Rarer, mostly in consulting and strategy, where the stakes can be measured.

    What moves a quote

    • ScopeThe number of pages, products or screens.
    • DesignFully custom, or built from your existing brand guidelines and mockups.
    • LanguagesEach language adds content; Arabic adds a right-to-left layout.
    • IntegrationsPayments, user accounts, third-party tools and APIs.
    • ContentCopy, photos and video: supplied by you, or produced.
    • TimelineA compressed schedule costs more: +25% in our estimator.

    06 — FAQ

    The questions that matter.

    What people ask before they start — answered straight.

    How long does a project take?

    It depends on the scope: 2 to 4 weeks for a showcase site, 3 to 7 for a custom site and 8 to 16 for a web application. You get a firm timeline as soon as we have agreed the scope together.

    What does it cost?

    Our packages start at 90,000 DZD for a showcase site (see Pricing for euros and dollars), and every project gets one fixed price once the scope is clear — no surprise fees. See also the Preview.

    What languages do you work in?

    French, Arabic and English — spoken, written, and in everything we deliver. This very site is trilingual, with a natively mirrored Arabic (right-to-left) experience.

    What happens after launch?

    We stay. Every package includes 1, 3 or 6 months of support, and we reply within 24 working hours. After that, maintenance is optional: monthly, with no lock-in (see Pricing). No “good luck” on launch day.

    Do you work remotely?

    Yes. We're based in Bordj Bou Arréridj, but we work with clients anywhere — including in France. Distance doesn't change your direct line to the person building your project.

    How are you different from an agency?

    No middleman: one team scopes, codes and ships your project, design to deploy.
