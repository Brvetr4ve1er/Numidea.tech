## KB-01-002 · Mission — *why we exist*

> **"We build the software your business has been waiting for."**

Numidea Labs exists to **make powerful, almost-magical technology accessible, safe,
and real for businesses** — designing, building, shipping, and maintaining digital
products with **no middlemen between the client and the people writing the code**.

- **Context:** Founded as a small senior team reacting against agency models where
  account managers, handoffs, and "telephone games" dilute the work. The wedge is
  *direct access to the builder* + *one team owning the whole chain*.
- **Importance:** This is the core promise that differentiates them; every service,
  brand decision, and piece of copy ladders up to it.
- **Sources:** twitter:description, README, hero copy. `confidence: verified`.

## KB-01-004 · Values & operating principles (verified from "How we work")

1. **You talk to the builder.** No account manager, no telephone game. Whoever
   scopes it, ships it.
2. **One team owns the whole chain.** Design → code → deploy. Nothing is lost in a
   handoff that never happens.
3. **We answer when it breaks.** Deadlines kept, replies < 24h. After launch,
   still there.

Implicit values evident in the work (`confidence: inferred`):
- **Proof over promises** — the signature offer is a *working demo before payment*;
  the site leads with receipts ("Real clients. Real deployments… open them").
- **Craft + discipline** — "one flourish per screen, then discipline"; 100
  Lighthouse, accessibility honored.
- **Honesty** — not-yet-live projects are labeled "Soon," never falsely "Live"
  (see Domain 13 decision).
