## KB-11-002 · IP guardrail — Arcanum brand (verified, important)
The Arcanum identity must remain an **original** evocation of a public design genre
(Art-Deco arcane-engineering), never a copy of any third party's protected
names/logos/characters/trade dress. Because Numidea is **commercial**, no fan-content
allowance applies. (Brand-strategy guidance, **not legal advice** — counsel should
clear the final identity.) See Domain 2 / Domain 13.
