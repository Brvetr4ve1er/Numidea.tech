# Domain 4 — Customer Knowledge

## KB-04-001 · Customer segments (inferred from portfolio)
1. **Local SMEs needing a credible web presence** — clinics, HVAC/industrial,
   travel agencies (e.g. Doctor Cherfia, almaflowclim, Alliance Travel).
2. **Local commerce/retail needing storefronts** — IPTV/streaming, PWA shops with
   WhatsApp checkout (Hamma Sat, Étoile de l'Est).
3. **Businesses needing automation / market intelligence** — lead pipelines,
   competitive/price intelligence, internal tools (the high-moat segment).
4. **Businesses needing strategy/growth** — audits, funnels, roadmaps.

`confidence: inferred` (derived from real projects; not a stated segmentation).

## KB-04-002 · Personas (assumed — to validate)
- **"Established-feel SME owner"** — wants to *look* established and bookable; values
  a calm, professional site and a single point of contact. Pain: agencies are slow,
  opaque, middleman-heavy.
- **"Local merchant"** — sells via WhatsApp/social; wants a storefront matching how
  Algerians actually buy. Pain: generic e-com doesn't fit local payment/cash habits.
- **"Operator drowning in manual work"** — needs scraping/automation to stop losing
  hours. Pain: no local studio offers real data engineering.

> 🔴 GAP: Personas are constructed from the work, not from interviews. `confidence:
> assumed`. Validate with real client interviews.

## KB-04-003 · Motivations / why clients choose Numidea (verified positioning)
Direct access to the builder; one team owning design→deploy; **a working demo before
they pay** ("The Preview"); proof/receipts; trilingual local fluency; <24h response.

## KB-04-004 · Pain points addressed (verified)
Agency "fat," handoffs/telephone-game, missed deadlines, no post-launch support,
guesswork instead of data, manual grind, generic non-local commerce.

## KB-04-005 · Customer journey (inferred)
Awareness (referral/local network/site) → **The Preview** (free working demo) →
"easiest yes" decision → build (one team) → launch → maintain ("answer when it
breaks"). Advocacy/referral loop: `unknown`.

## KB-04-006 · Retention / churn drivers (inferred)
- **Retention:** post-launch responsiveness, full-chain ownership, direct relationship.
- **Churn risk:** small team capacity limits; the contact form currently **doesn't
  deliver leads** (Domain 7/14) — silent top-of-funnel loss.
