## Type system (the owner's direction: gaming/anime UI typography)

- Spec: https://claude.ai/artifact/BrvX62HC49TTPheF3Yiwhw. The open question in it is whether to retire the Cinzel Gravure plates.
- Four roles:
  - DISPLAY: Archivo Condensed 900, caps
  - UI: Archivo Expanded 800, caps
  - LABEL: Geist Mono, caps, tracked
  - BODY: Geist
- Arabic uses Reem Kufi for display and UI, and Plex Arabic for labels and body, with no caps or tracking.
- Tokens live in `hub/style.css`: `--f-cond`, `--f-wide`, `--fs-micro` to `--fs-index`, `--lh-*`, `--tr-*`.
- `hub/fonts/hud.css` is generated by `scripts/build-hub.mjs`. The Lanterne pages link it (`scripts/lib/lanterne.mjs`, "the sheet").
- The audit that motivated it found 193 distinct font sizes across 3 sheets, 2 competing token sets, and 3 display faces.
