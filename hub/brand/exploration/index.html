Turn 1 · brand enhancement · exploration

Louder. Same house.

The brief was to borrow the energy of a louder reference without borrowing its marks: flat two-colour, condensed display type, a mascot emblem and stickers. Everything is built from what Numidea already had: the studio orange #F93E06 of the earlier Engineering surface, a condensed face from the type library, and the cursor block of the wordmark.

The idea, in one line

Run the brand in two gears.

  • Arcanum stays the site and sales register, because calm sells.
  • Loud would be the social, video and merch register, because it stops the scroll.

This was a proposal at the time and was not taken up as the identity.

The loud kit

Exploration

Display · a condensed face from the type library

Numidea. Built loud.

The library face is named Bloxter Condensed. It is not among the site’s font files, so it is drawn here in Archivo Condensed as a stand-in. Geist Mono sets every label and Geist the body.

Rules of the gear

  • Two colours and white. Arcane blue and brass stay in Arcanum.
  • Type is set tight and huge, in uppercase. Headlines still end on a full stop.
  • Stickers can rotate ±8°. Screens never rotate.
05PL. 05

Mascot directions

Superseded

Three concept marks, to choose between.

Superseded These were direction marks, not final art. The studio’s cast is now Yasser and Kūro, as set out in plate 16 of the adopted identity. They stay here as a record of the road not taken.

Fenek, the fennec.

Algeria’s national animal, small and sharp, drawn as a flat emblem mask. The giant ears carry the story: it hears you, which is the studio’s promise (no middlemen, we answer) told without a word.

Personality
Alert, dry-witted, unbothered. It never panics.
In motion
The ears flick when a message lands, the eyes narrow to scan, then a nod and it ships.
Risk
Fox mascots are common. The ear silhouette and the angled eye cut would have to own it.

Bloc, the cursor.

The blinking block from the wordmark, alive. It is already the one animated part of the brand, so it is the cheapest mascot to own and the most “software”.

Personality
Eager, tireless, a bit literal. It types everything it hears.
In motion
It blinks, then walks the text on as it types it. It stretches into a loading bar and squashes into a full stop.
Risk
It is abstract and has no roots. Numidia disappears.

Hexo, the engine.

A hexagon head with an aperture eye, built from the site’s honeycomb and its Preview glyph. It is the automation work as a character: a machine that watches so you don’t have to.

Personality
Calm, precise, quietly smug about uptime.
In motion
The aperture dilates on focus, the antenna pings on every new lead, and the hex rotates one face per step.
Risk
Robots read as cold. It would need warmth from the writing.

Any of the three would have needed a proper illustration pass: an expression sheet, a turnaround, and the emblem tested from favicon size to a building-side banner.

One rule change left open. Arcanum says “nothing bounces”. The loud gear would need a little squash on stickers and the mascot (S2, S3), or it feels stiff. The idea was to limit it to 2 frames, on characters and stickers only, and never on interface or type.
